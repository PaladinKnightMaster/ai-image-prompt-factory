## Interpretation

In this prompt context, explicit posture mechanics did not improve average pose quality over generic pose language. The explicit variant scored 0.25 lower on the primary `pose_mechanics` dimension and 0.125 lower overall. It also showed greater run-to-run variability.

This result should **not** be interpreted as proof that explicit pose mechanics are generally harmful. With only four outputs per variant and one frozen review set, the appropriate conclusion is that this run **weakly contradicts** the claim of general superiority.
