## Hypothesis

> Removing the phrase `8K masterpiece` does not materially reduce visible photographic rendering quality in a production-like editorial fashion prompt.

## Experimental contrast

The control ended with:

> `8K masterpiece, ultra-detailed.`

The variant removed only the `8K masterpiece` phrase and preserved the detail instruction:

> `Ultra-detailed.`

The adult subject, gallery corridor, mid-turn action, deep teal satin evening dress, structured clutch, hand logic, side-window daylight, layered spatial composition, 85mm portrait perspective, aspect ratio, and all host generation settings were held constant.

## Interpretation

Removing `8K masterpiece` did not change the primary `photographic_rendering_quality` score, semantic compliance, or technical-defect cleanliness. The no-hype condition was, however, **0.50 lower on aesthetic quality** and **0.125 lower overall**.

The run is therefore classified **weakly_supports** rather than `supports`. In this richer editorial-fashion Transfer Bench context, the generic quality-hype phrase appears unnecessary for the core visible photographic-rendering outcome, but the secondary aesthetic decrease leaves open the possibility of a small context-specific aesthetic contribution. Replication is required before treating `8K masterpiece` as generally redundant.
