# EXP-015 Result - Post-Hoc Negative vs Pre-Resolved Historical Content

**Experiment version:** `1.1.0`
**Benchmark layer:** Transfer Bench
**Archetype:** `TB-A03`
**Model target:** `gpt-image-2`
**Model snapshot:** `unknown`
**Replicates:** 4 per variant
**Review protocol:** second-layer blind review with precommitted mapping
**Evidence classification:** **inconclusive**

## Hypothesis

> Resolving an anachronistic object into a period-compatible positive instruction before composition improves historical-cultural coherence compared with retaining the conflicting modern object and relying on post-hoc negative prompting.

## Experimental contrast

The control retained:

> `a compact electric desk lamp`

while also retaining:

> `Avoid modern electrical objects and anachronistic lighting fixtures.`

The variant replaced only the conflicting positive object with:

> `a ceramic oil lamp`

The final negative constraint and all other registered scene content were held
constant.

## Interpretation

The pre-resolved-positive-content condition did not improve the registered
primary `historical_cultural_coherence` dimension.

Both conditions scored **4.00**, producing a primary delta of **0.00**.

The variant showed a small secondary aesthetic-quality increase from **4.50**
to **4.75**. Semantic compliance and technical cleanliness were unchanged.

The overall mean increased from **4.6250** to **4.6875**, a difference of
**+0.0625**.

EXP-015 is classified **inconclusive**.

The primary score was not ceiling-saturated, but all eight outputs nevertheless
received the same primary score. This run therefore did not discriminate
between upstream positive conflict resolution and post-hoc negative prompting.

A zero observed difference does not establish equivalence.

## Limitations

- Four replicates per condition are insufficient for a statistical-significance claim.
- Only one frozen blind-review score set was used.
- The exact host model snapshot and reproducible generation seed were unavailable.
- The result is specific to the `TB-A03` Ming-period scholar-study context.
- Every replicate in both conditions received a primary score of **4**.
- Shared historical-authenticity weaknesses unrelated to the manipulated lamp phrase may have reduced treatment sensitivity.
- A zero observed primary difference does not establish equivalence.
