# EXP-011 Result - Generic Garment Description vs Explicit Garment Construction

**Run:** `EXP-011-1.1.2-20260914T204222Z-W883`  
**Experiment version:** `1.1.2`  
**Benchmark layer:** Transfer Bench  
**Archetype:** `TB-A01` Editorial Fashion Motion Portrait  
**Model target:** `gpt-image-2`  
**Model snapshot:** `unknown`  
**Replicates:** 4 per variant  
**Review protocol:** second-layer blind review with precommitted mapping  
**Evidence classification:** **weakly_supports**

## Hypothesis

> Detailed garment-construction language improves visible wardrobe-construction fidelity over a generic garment description in a production-like fashion portrait.

## Experimental contrast

The control retained the generic garment description:

> `deep teal satin evening dress`

The variant replaced only that garment span with explicit construction geometry:

> `deep teal satin evening dress with narrow shoulder straps, a clean neckline, princess seams shaping the bodice, a defined waist seam, and a clean finished hem`

The adult subject, gallery corridor, mid-turn action, deep teal color, satin material, evening-dress category, structured clutch, lighting, spatial composition, 85mm portrait perspective, and host generation settings were held constant.

Fabric-motion, drape, weight, fold-response, and other fabric-physics language were intentionally excluded from the treatment so EXP-011 remained distinct from EXP-012.

## Interpretation

The explicit-garment-construction condition modestly improved the primary `garment_construction_fidelity` dimension.

It scored **5.00** versus **4.75** for the generic-garment control, a **+0.25** difference. Semantic compliance, aesthetic quality, and technical-defect cleanliness were unchanged between conditions.

The overall difference was small at **+0.0625**, so EXP-011 is classified **weakly_supports**, not `supports`.

This result does not establish that more garment-detail wording is universally better. It shows that, in this `TB-A01` editorial-fashion context, concrete construction geometry produced a small improvement in visible garment structure without reducing the other registered dimensions.

## Limitations

- Four replicates per condition are insufficient for a statistical-significance claim.
- Only one frozen blind-review score set was used, so inter-rater reliability was not measured.
- The exact host model snapshot and reproducible generation seed were unavailable.
- The finding is specific to `TB-A01`.
- The primary-dimension improvement was small at 0.25 points.
- The result applies to the tested construction geometry and does not establish a general rule for all garment-detail language.
