## Hypothesis

> A readable natural-language production brief preserves multi-constraint scene fidelity at least as well as semantically equivalent JSON-style structured syntax.

## Experimental contrast

The control used the `TB-A05` scene as a readable natural-language production brief.

The variant encoded the same subject, action, environment, lighting, composition, rendering, medium, and exclusions as JSON-style structured text.

The adult courier, rain-darkened rooftop market, blue-hour setting, folded map tube, layered travel coat and practical boots, distant signal lantern, wind-caught coat hem, three-quarter full-body framing, asymmetric composition, foreground awning edges, midground courier action, background rooflines, painterly rendering treatment, material separation, warm/cool lighting relationship, focal hierarchy, exclusions, and host generation settings were held constant.

Only the prompt representation format was intentionally changed.

## Interpretation

No visible prompt-format effect was detected in this run.

Both the readable natural-language brief and the semantically matched JSON-style representation received maximum scores on every registered dimension. The primary `scene_constraint_fidelity` score was **5.00** for both conditions, and the overall mean was **5.0000** for both conditions.

This is **not** treated as evidence that the two formats are equivalent.

All eight outputs saturated the 5-point review scale, so the evaluation had no remaining discriminatory range with which to detect a format advantage. EXP-010 is therefore classified **inconclusive**, rather than `supports`, `weakly_supports`, or evidence of superiority for either format.

The result does show that, in this particular `TB-A05` run, neither representation produced an observable failure relative to the other under the registered review dimensions.

## Limitations

- Four replicates per condition are insufficient for a statistical-significance claim.
- Only one frozen blind-review score set was used, so inter-rater reliability was not measured.
- The exact host model snapshot and reproducible generation seed were unavailable.
- The finding is specific to `TB-A05`.
- Every output received the maximum score on every registered dimension, producing a complete ceiling effect.
- A zero observed difference under a ceiling-saturated review does not establish prompt-format equivalence.
