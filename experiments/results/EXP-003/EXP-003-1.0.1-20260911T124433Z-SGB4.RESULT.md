## Interpretation

In this refined-interior portrait context, temporal-causal micro-story framing improved the primary `visual_event_coherence` score by 1.50 over a semantically matched static description. Aesthetic quality improved by 0.75 and the overall mean improved by 0.50, while semantic compliance was unchanged. Technical-defect cleanliness decreased slightly by 0.25. The micro-story group was also more consistent across the four replicates.

This run therefore **supports** the tested hypothesis in this context. It should not be interpreted as proof that micro-story framing is universally superior: four outputs per condition and one frozen review set are not enough for a statistical-significance claim or broad generalization.

## Pattern implication

`narrative-micro-action` should move from `observed` to `supported`, become `compiler_eligible: true`, and be enabled with `director_gate: true` for its existing routes.

The pattern already had repeated internal-corpus observations. EXP-003 adds direct controlled blinded evidence that a small temporal-causal action/reaction can make an otherwise matched portrait feel more like a coherent captured event than a static pose. This promotion is intentionally scoped: the empirical evidence is still one small-N portrait run and should be replicated across other actions, environments, subjects, props, and routes.

## Limitations

- Four replicates per variant; no statistical-significance claim is warranted.
- One frozen review score set; inter-rater reliability was not measured.
- Semantic compliance was at the 1-5 scale ceiling for both conditions.
- The host exposed `gpt-image-2` but no exact model snapshot or reproducible generation seed.
- The finding is specific to one refined-interior portrait scenario with a window-directed reaction and ceramic vase.
- Replication across other actions, environments, subjects, props, and routes is required before broader generalization.
