## Blind review

Two independent blind reviewers score each image **individually**, never side by side. Reviewer-visible context may identify a Japanese woodblock print and the upper sky as the designated effect region; it must not say `bokashi`. Hide treatment, prompt text and hashes, generation IDs, replicate IDs, and private mapping. Freeze both independent review records before revealing the committed mapping. The per-image primary score is the arithmetic mean of the two reviewers' primary scores. There is no post-reveal adjudication.

The registered dimensions are primary `art_material_fidelity`, then `semantic_compliance`, `aesthetic_quality`, and `technical_defects` (higher means cleaner). This no-transformation fixture has no transformation-fidelity, identity, or artifact-integration score.

### Primary art/material rubric

| Score | Anchor |
| --- | --- |
| 5 | Clearly localized darker-to-lighter gradation in the target sky; visually integrated as a printed pigment field; surrounding regions remain predominantly flat; no meaningful global photographic/3D shading or obvious airbrushed overlay. |
| 4 | Clear localized print-like gradation with one noticeable minor weakness, such as slightly over-smooth rendering, minor spill, or imperfect integration. |
| 3 | Gradation exists in the target region but is materially ambiguous between print-like gradation and generic digital shading, or localization/integration is inconsistent. |
| 2 | Weak/barely identifiable target gradation, or primarily generic/global/3D-like shading. |

## Locked analysis

Compute the mean of the four reviewer-averaged primary scores in each condition. Report B−C and B−A separately. Apply the V3.3 descriptive bands to each delta: `supports` at ≥+1.0; `weakly_supports` at +0.5 to <+1.0; `inconclusive` at >−0.5 to <+0.5; `weakly_contradicts` at >−1.0 to ≤−0.5; `contradicts` at ≤−1.0. These are small-N descriptive rules, not statistical-significance thresholds.

For each contrast, compare all 16 cross-condition pairs of reviewer-averaged primary scores. Exclude ties. If the mean-delta direction does not win a strict majority of non-tied pairs, move that contrast **one evidence level toward inconclusive**. Report pair wins, losses, and ties.

The full hypothesis may be supported only when **both** B−C and B−A are at least weakly positive, **at least one** reaches `supports`, and no registered safeguard, provenance, or side-effect veto fires. Mixed directions or one positive and one neutral contrast are experiment-level `inconclusive`; report the contrasts separately rather than averaging them away.

Apply the following caps and veto before the final classification:

- **Ceiling:** if all four C reviewer-averaged primary scores are ≥4.5, B−C superiority cannot be supported; cap overall classification at `inconclusive`.
- **Floor:** if all three condition means are ≤2.0 and no reviewer-averaged sample exceeds 3.0, cap final classification at `inconclusive`.
- **Reviewer ambiguity:** if reviewers differ by ≥2 primary-score points on at least 4 of 12 samples, cap final classification at `inconclusive`.
- **Side effects:** if B's condition mean is ≥1.0 point worse than either comparator on `semantic_compliance`, `aesthetic_quality`, or `technical_defects` cleanliness, overall evidence cannot exceed `inconclusive`.
- **Provenance:** missing required bindings prevent a supportive experiment-level conclusion even if a score signal appears.

No rerolls are allowed for decodable outputs. A technical infrastructure failure before an evaluable image may receive at most two retries, with every attempt preserved. After three failed technical attempts on one slot, stop the run as incomplete. Record provider safety refusals; do not silently reroll them.
