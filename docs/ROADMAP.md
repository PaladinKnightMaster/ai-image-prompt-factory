# Capability Roadmap

These are planning targets, not promises or evidence that a feature exists. The [current state](PROJECT_STATE.md) identifies what has actually shipped. Older [V3.1](V3.1_ROADMAP.md), [V3.2](V3.2_ROADMAP.md), and [V3.3](V3_3_ROADMAP.md) roadmaps remain historical planning records, not this forward-looking capability index.

## Completed

### V3.3 - Empirical Lab

**Closed.** Added preregistered experiments, frozen fixtures, controlled review, execution provenance, result classification, replication, transfer testing, and cross-experiment synthesis. Findings and limits are in the [V3.3 evidence synthesis](V3.3_EVIDENCE_SYNTHESIS.md).

## Planned

### V3.4 - Transformation Intelligence

Candidate composable routes: identity → era; identity → historical role; identity → artifact; identity → art method; era → art method; artifact → living reconstruction; living scene → artifact; historical → modern encounter. Routes should compose shared identity, era, art-method, artifact, and evidence knowledge rather than duplicate prompt libraries. Research questions are in the [queue](RESEARCH_QUEUE.md).

### V3.5 - Visual Knowledge Expansion

Candidate focus: deeper ArtMethodPack and EraPack libraries, ArtifactPack expansion, evidence-backed compatibility knowledge, and stronger coverage of sculpture, ceramics, painting, print, paper, textile, and architectural/cave/tomb art.

### V3.6 - Gallery & Discovery Experience

Candidate focus: richer public gallery, filtering and discovery, golden cases, case audits, transformation comparisons, and reusable sample input/output pairs.

### V4.0 - Mature Agentic Visual Director

High-level target: a coherent, evidence-aware visual director that composes transformation, historical/art knowledge, empirical evaluation, and reusable case discovery. Scope and release criteria require later decisions.
