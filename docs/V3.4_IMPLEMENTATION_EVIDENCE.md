## Four resolver and compiler examples

Every resolver receives `portrait` as the source representation and uses the same pack schema and resolver. The representation deltas specify geometry, depth, and surface mapping; the selected method, era, form, state, and compatibility gates remain separate.

| Candidate | Transformation resolver target and defining mapping | Resolved concept IDs | Compiled candidate summary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Greek marble bust | `portrait_to_volumetric_sculpture` → `volumetric_sculpture`; portrait becomes a supported three-dimensional object | `method.marble_sculpture` | 2,535 characters; Greek context, bust geometry, carved stone realization; no contrapposto or polychromy |
| Bounded Dunhuang mural | `portrait_to_architectural_mural` → `architectural_mural`; portrait becomes wall-bound planar imagery | `method.mineral_pigment_mural`, `mural.prepared_earthen_plaster`, `mural.ground_layer`, `mural.line_drawing`, `mural.mineral_pigment_fields`, `mural.wall_integration` | 3,132 characters; bounded Mogao context, wall support, layered mural realization; no pouncing |
| Edo woodblock print | `portrait_to_relief_print` → `relief_print`; portrait becomes contours and bounded color regions on a sheet | `method.japanese_woodblock_print`, `woodblock.keyblock`, `woodblock.color_block`, `woodblock.kento_registration`, `woodblock.baren_printing`, `woodblock.kozo_paper` | 2,833 characters; Edo context, physical print sheet, carved/printed realization; no bokashi |
| Blue-and-white vase | `portrait_to_decorated_artifact` → `decorated_artifact`; portrait wraps the selected object's zones and curvature | `method.blue_white_ceramic`, `ceramic.cobalt_pigment`, `ceramic.underglaze_decoration`, `ceramic.transparent_glaze`, `ceramic.reserved_white_ground`, `ceramic.vessel_zones`, `ceramic.curvature_aware_decoration` | 2,702 characters; vase geometry and underglaze layering; no EraPack, Ming inference, or unselected porcelain-body concept |

The four actual `lexical_trace` examples below are each the first emitted record for their candidate. Each trace also carries `source_component: art_method`, the preferred term, and an evidence source ID. The complete traces are returned by `compile_result` for each candidate.

| Candidate | Lexical profile | Preferred term | Realized text | Evidence source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Greek bust | `historical_precision` | marble sculpture | marble sculpture, human form translated into continuous carved stone mass and surface rather than stone-colored living skin; carved planar transitions | `met-greek-art-resource` |
| Dunhuang mural | `artifact_technical` | mineral-pigment mural | mineral-pigment mural, painting materially bound to a prepared wall through drawn contours and pigment layers rather than a framed digital image | `getty-mogao-wall-conservation` |
| Edo print | `artifact_technical` | Japanese woodblock print | Japanese woodblock print, relief-printed image built from carved contours and separate color impressions on paper | `met-ukiyoe-process` |
| Ceramic vase | `artifact_technical` | blue-and-white ceramic | blue-and-white ceramic, cobalt decoration integrated with a light ceramic body beneath glaze, without implying a particular dynasty | `vam-blue-white-ceramics-2024` |

The realizer accepts only resolved IDs and modifiers, reads lexicon entries, and emits deterministic clauses plus one trace entry per emitted concept. It makes no domain inference or LLM call. Legacy prompt fragments remain on the non-V3.4 path. No entire lexicon is dumped: the candidates emit 1, 6, 6, and 7 concepts respectively. Conditional concepts remain absent unless explicitly selected and gated; tests cover both rejection and authorized selection. EXP-019 remains a planned C/A/B definition with no generations, scores, or superiority claim.
