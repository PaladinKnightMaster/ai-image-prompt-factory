# ADR-0003 — V3.2 Visual Intelligence Lab

V3.2 separates historical/material evidence from empirical prompt-mechanism knowledge. Source prompts and images are observations, not proof of causal effectiveness. Internal corpus examples are decomposed into candidate mechanisms; VisualPatterns become compiler-eligible only after explicit validation status. Controlled experiments are first-class records, and planned experiments are never reported as executed.

The 606-image corpus remains internal-only. The public release contains indexes, derived mechanism metadata, experiment definitions, and first-party benchmark plans, but not the raw source images.
