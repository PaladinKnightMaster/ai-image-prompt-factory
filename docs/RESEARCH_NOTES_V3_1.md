# V3.1 Research Notes

V3.1 prioritized primary/high-quality museum, heritage, and specialist sources rather than prompt galleries for factual claims.

## Tang ceramics

The evidence model separates early/rare Tang cobalt decoration from the later flourishing/standardization of blue-and-white porcelain associated especially with Yuan/Jingdezhen production. This distinction powers the four strictness acceptance test rather than treating every blue-and-white request as simply “wrong” or “right.”

## Dunhuang

Mogao is modeled as a multi-century site with cave/date narrowing and technical pigment/material evidence. Artifact state and pigment discoloration/restoration must remain separate from an imagined in-period appearance.

## Greek sculpture

Surviving white marble is not automatically treated as original unpainted appearance; polychromy/survival bias is represented explicitly. Bronze fabrication is modeled independently through hollow lost-wax casting, joining/cold work/inlays and material survival.

## Roman bronze

Roman portrait bronze evidence informs bust conventions and metal/inlay/patina behavior rather than inheriting marble logic.

## Edo

Arita/Hizen porcelain and underglaze cobalt production are distinguished from ukiyo-e woodblock production. Ukiyo-e includes designer/engraver/printer/publisher workflow, separate blocks, registration and paper/ink behavior.
