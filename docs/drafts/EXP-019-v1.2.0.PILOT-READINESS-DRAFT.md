# EXP-019 v1.2.0 — Non-Experimental Pilot Readiness DRAFT

> **DRAFT — NON-EXECUTABLE.** Prospective criteria for owner review. Not
> frozen, not registered, authorizes nothing — including no pilot.

## 1. Purpose

A pilot exists to prove the v1.2 evidence workflow end to end — direct-paste
delivery, contemporaneous captures, action logging, digest binding, and the
admission decision policy — without producing any experimental evidence. A
pilot is **not** a small experiment: its material can never enter the
experimental sample, its images are never scored, and nothing learned from
pilot outputs may alter the preserved hypothesis, design, rubric, or
analysis.

## 2. Entry criteria (all required)

1. Owner approval of proposed decisions H1, H2, and H3.
2. A frozen, version-pinned v1.2 runtime enforcing every runtime-reserved
   check in the draft evidence schemas.
3. That runtime passing the merged synthetic fixture suite, including the
   invalid, contradictory, no-output, interrupted/unknown, and
   digest-mismatch scenarios.
4. Leak scanning demonstrated on the pilot's own public artifacts.
5. A written pilot plan using only non-experimental material: prompts and
   content that are not the experimental fixture, its treatments, or its
   scene, and identifiers drawn from a disjoint synthetic set.

## 3. Conduct boundaries

- Non-experimental prompts only; no experimental prompt bytes, treatment
  clauses, or scene content, in whole or in part.
- Pilot artifacts are stored outside any experimental run root, labelled
  as pilot material in every artifact, and excluded from experimental
  counts, checkpoints, and analysis.
- No pilot output is reviewer-scored, compared, or used to tune prompts,
  workflow wording that touches experimental content, or any scientific
  parameter.
- The pilot exercises the operator workflow exactly as drafted: direct
  paste, no attachment, request capture at submission, response capture on
  appearance, unchanged download, hashing, action-log closure, and
  attestation where applicable.

## 4. Exit criteria

The pilot is complete only when the record shows:

- each pilot invocation reached exactly one terminal disposition under the
  draft decision policy, with its evidence bundle complete;
- digest recomputation and cross-artifact binding verified by the runtime,
  including at least one deliberately corrupted artifact correctly rejected
  (using synthetic corruption created for the pilot, outside any
  experimental material);
- missing-telemetry and unsolicited class-D cases handled without any model
  metadata query or recovery step;
- no experimental identifier, prompt, or allocation material present in any
  pilot artifact, verified by leak scan;
- a pilot report listing every defect found and its disposition, with any
  workflow change confined to evidence handling and re-reviewed before any
  experimental freeze.

Failure of any exit criterion means the workflow returns to draft revision;
it never means relaxing a criterion to accommodate the pilot's results.
Any workflow change made in response to the pilot — however small — must
re-run the synthetic fixture validation before proceeding, and a change
that alters evidence-collection behaviour (what is captured, when, or how
it is bound) additionally requires a repeat pilot and fresh owner
acceptance at gate G3 before any experimental freeze.
