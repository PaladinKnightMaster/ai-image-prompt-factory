# EXP-019 v1.2 — Implementation and Freeze Sequencing DRAFT (PR-5–PR-15)

> **DRAFT — NON-EXECUTABLE.** This is a proposed work sequence for owner
> review. It implements nothing, freezes nothing, and authorizes no pilot,
> no allocation, and no generation. EXP-019 v1.2 remains unregistered in
> `frozen_protocol()` until Stage 4 below, and no stage may be skipped or
> reordered.

## Gate model

Work proceeds through five stages separated by explicit owner gates. A
later stage may not begin until the preceding gate is recorded as passed.
At every point before Stage 5, the code base must be **incapable of
starting an experimental run**: no run-root creation, no allocation
generation, and no generation tasking may be reachable, and any runtime
built in Stage 2 operates on synthetic or pilot material only.

## Stage 1 — Approval of the engineering contract (gate G1)

- **PR-5:** Engineering-contract gate record: it documents the owner's
  2026-10-22 approval of decisions H1, H2, and H3 and of the PR-4 draft
  artifacts, and accepts the runtime-reserved validation list from the
  draft schemas as the engineering contract. It approves no scientific
  activation, randomization, or generation.
- **Gate G1:** passed when the PR-5 decision record is merged. The H1–H3
  approvals themselves were explicitly granted by the owner on 2026-10-22
  (silence would not have been approval); PR-5 is their formal record.
  Until G1 is recorded, everything remains draft and non-executable.

## Stage 2 — Implementation and synthetic validation (gate G2)

Each PR is independently reviewed and merged; none registers an executable
experiment.

- **PR-6:** Runtime evidence validator (schema plus every runtime-reserved
  cross-artifact, temporal, digest, and authentication check), exercised
  only against the synthetic fixture suite.
- **PR-7:** Attempt lifecycle state machine and terminal attempt receipts,
  including exactly-once local/external commitment states, tested
  synthetically.
- **PR-8:** External provenance anchoring for attempt receipts and the
  terminal `run_disposition`, preserving the public six-field checkpoint
  format, tested against a disposable test remote.
- **PR-9:** Admission decision engine implementing the draft decision
  precedence, retry narrowing, and aggregate class-D conflict counting.
- **PR-10:** Operator-workflow tooling for the direct-paste/no-attachment
  procedure (captures, action log, attestation support), with no pathway
  that attaches files or solicits model metadata.
- **PR-11:** Reviewer-package builder and role/coordinator tasking support
  enforcing the access boundaries and treatment-free package contents.
- **PR-12:** Integrated synthetic end-to-end validation: the full pipeline
  over synthetic bundles, deliberate-corruption rejection, leak scanning of
  every public artifact (files and rendered patches), and a dry-run
  activation lockout test proving no experimental run can start.
- **Gate G2:** all Stage-2 PRs merged, the synthetic suite and leak scans
  green, and the activation lockout demonstrated.

## Stage 3 — Pre-fixed non-experimental pilot (gate G3)

- **PR-13:** Execution and reporting of the non-experimental pilot under
  the pilot readiness draft: non-experimental prompts only, disjoint
  synthetic identifiers, material stored outside any run root, nothing
  scored, nothing entering experimental counts.
- **Gate G3 — pilot acceptance:** the owner accepts or rejects the pilot
  report against the pilot exit criteria. Any workflow change the pilot
  motivates is confined to evidence handling, returns through Stage-2-style
  review, and is re-validated synthetically before proceeding; a change
  altering evidence-collection behaviour additionally requires a repeat
  pilot and fresh G3 acceptance.

## Stage 4 — Final scientific preregistration and randomization freeze (gate G4)

- **PR-14:** The final v1.2 preregistration and definition are frozen:
  preserved scientific content re-verified by digest against the v1.1.1
  sources; H1–H3 incorporated as approved; v1.2 registered in
  `frozen_protocol()` as a frozen-but-not-yet-activated protocol; the
  experimental allocation (opaque identifiers, order, mapping) generated
  privately by the allocation custodian (see the roles draft), committed
  only to the private provenance store, and verified absent from every
  public artifact by leak scan.
- **Gate G4:** independent review of the freeze diff confirms the science
  is byte-identical in substance to the preserved sources, the allocation
  exists only privately, and the activation lockout still prevents any run
  from starting without Stage 5.

## Stage 5 — First real experimental generation (gate G5)

- **PR-15:** Activation: creation of the experimental run root under the
  private store, anchoring of the run root checkpoint, and authorization
  of the first real generation under the frozen protocol. Only now may an
  invocation be started.
- **Gate G5:** the owner explicitly authorizes activation after G1–G4 are
  recorded. No earlier PR, merge, or document may be read as this
  authorization.

## Invariants across all stages

- The v1.1.1 run is never modified, closed, or used as pilot or v1.2
  material.
- Historical-preservation, draft-schema, and synthetic-fixture suites stay
  green at every merge.
- Any change to the preserved hypothesis, treatments, design, rubric, or
  analysis stops the sequence and requires separate owner authorization —
  it is never a "documentation fix."
- A failed gate returns work to the relevant stage; gates are never waived
  retroactively.
