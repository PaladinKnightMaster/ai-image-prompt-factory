## 1. Gate definition

Per `docs/drafts/EXP-019-v1.2.0.FREEZE-SEQUENCING-DRAFT.md`, Stage 1 /
gate G1 is the approval of the engineering contract: adoption of the PR-4
draft artifacts and of decisions H1, H2, and H3, with the draft schemas'
runtime-reserved validation list accepted as the engineering contract.
G1 is passed when this record is merged. Until then, everything remains
draft and non-executable.

## 4. Effect of G1 passing

On merge of this record, Stage-2 engineering preparation (PR-6 onward) may
be *proposed*, one PR at a time, each independently reviewed. G1 passing
changes nothing about the experiment itself:

- EXP-019 v1.2 remains unregistered and cannot be executed.
- No allocation exists; none may be created before gate G4, and then only
  by the allocation custodian into the private provenance store.
- No run root or experimental checkpoint exists or is created by this PR.
- The pilot (G3), final scientific freeze (G4), and activation (G5) remain
  separate future gates, each requiring its own explicit authorization.
